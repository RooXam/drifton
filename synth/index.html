Synth

Two oscillators with unison, noise, a filter, two envelopes, and two LFOs you can plug into the sound through the modulation matrix, and its own effects: a chorus, and how much goes to the room and the echo. Play with the middle row of your computer keyboard (black keys on the row above), or click the keys. Z / X shift the octave (W / X on AZERTY). Each instrument keeps its own sound: with Hold on, leave a chord ringing on one and play another over it. Changes are saved in your browser and used by the generator.
