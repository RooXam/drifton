Drifton

Small sound tools that run in your browser: listen, play, learn, make.

Listen

Focus music

Endless music composed live, to focus, relax or drift off: moods from a soft arpeggio to a still drone.

Play & learn

Piano score

Practice from a MIDI file: slow it down, loop a passage.

Songbook

Songs to play on guitar: the chords of each part, the shapes, play along.

Chords

A guessing game to learn guitar chords: strum the shape, ask for hints.

Make

Chord progressions

Progressions typical of a style, found in 680,000 songs. Listen, transpose, export MIDI.

Synth

Play and shape the sounds Drifton uses.

Sketch

Loops of drums, bass, keys and pad, launched by scene, played on pads in key. Send them to Ableton Live as MIDI.

Lab

Pad states

Crossfade your own audio files with a conductor and a granular layer.
