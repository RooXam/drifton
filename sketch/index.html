Sketch

A clip starts at the next bar and loops; one clip per track. The buttons on the right launch a whole row (a scene). Numbers 1–8 launch scenes, space plays and stops.

Empty slot. Start a clip of

or press Record and play the pads, or play first and press Capture.

Pads in key: rows a fourth apart, roots lit. With Record on, what you play goes into the clip playing on this track. Capture turns your last phrase into a clip. On a computer, keys A to L play too.

Song

Key and scale set the pads and the rows of the piano roll. 100 % swing is a triplet shuffle. Volume, space and echo belong to this device, not to the project.

Tracks

The sounds come from the synth's starter sounds; the drums are synthesized too, no samples.

To Ableton Live

One MIDI track per instrument, named, at the project tempo; drums on channel 10 with General MIDI notes, so a Drum Rack plays them as they are. On a phone the share sheet opens: AirDrop the file to your Mac and drop it into Live.

Projects

Projects are saved on this device as you go (nothing leaves it). Export a project to keep a copy or move it to another device.
