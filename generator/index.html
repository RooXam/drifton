Focus music

Endless music composed live, to focus or relax: a pad holding the chords, a bass, a soft piano playing little arpeggios, sparse bells and a texture, in a room with echo. Layers come and go slowly; nothing ever repeats exactly.

Mood

Harmony

Key and mode changes apply from the next chord. Chords change at the start of a bar.

Instruments

Movement: how much the keys and the bass play in rhythm (0 = held chords only). Edit opens the instrument on the synth page in a new tab: your changes are heard here right away.

Sound

Space: the room (reverb). Echo: repeats in time with the tempo. Warmth: softer highs and a gentle saturation. Tape: an old cassette, the pitch slowly wobbling, hiss. Pulse: the volume shimmers several times a second, like Brain.fm's focus music (16 Hz for focus, slower for calm); keep it subtle.
