Sound

"Synth pad" plays the Pad and Bass sounds of the synth, as you saved them. The chord notes are violet, the bass blue. In the guitar view you hear the notes of the chord shapes, strummed. The Push view is the chromatic note layout in fourths (Push 1, 2, 3): scale pads lit softly, the tonic a bit more.

Data: Chordonomicon (CC BY-NC 4.0), analysed with Python (how). Keys are guessed from the chords, so a few numerals may be off.
