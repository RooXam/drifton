Sound

"Synth pad" plays the Pad and Bass sounds of the synth, as you saved them. The chord notes are violet, the bass blue. In the guitar view you hear the notes of the chord shapes, strummed. The Push view is the chromatic note layout in fourths (Push 1, 2, 3): scale pads lit softly, the tonic a bit more; or the In Key layout, scale notes only.

Voicings (piano, Push and MIDI export): Close packs the notes and moves each chord as little as possible; Open spreads every other note an octave up; Drop 2 lowers the second note from the top an octave, the jazz way; Shell leaves the root to the bass and plays the third, the seventh and the colours.

Data: Chordonomicon (CC BY-NC 4.0), analysed with Python (how). Keys are guessed from the chords, so a few numerals may be off.
